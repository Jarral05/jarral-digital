SELF-HOSTED FONTS GO HERE.

Why not load them from Google?
Because Google Fonts sends your visitor's IP address to Google. A German
court ruled in 2022 that doing this without consent breaches GDPR. You are
selling to EU clients. Self-host.

HOW TO GET THE FILES (5 minutes, free):

1. Open  https://gwfh.mranftl.com/fonts
2. Search for "Space Grotesk". Tick weights 600 and 700.
3. Under "Copy CSS", choose "Modern Browsers" (woff2 only).
4. Download the zip. Put the .woff2 files in THIS folder.
5. Repeat for "IBM Plex Sans", weights 400, 500 and 600.
6. Rename them to match the @font-face rules at the top of
   assets/css/main.css, for example:
       space-grotesk-600.woff2
       space-grotesk-700.woff2
       ibm-plex-sans-400.woff2
       ibm-plex-sans-500.woff2
       ibm-plex-sans-600.woff2

That is it. The CSS already points at these filenames.
